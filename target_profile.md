# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) - Ahluwalia Strategic Ventures

## Segment 1: Small/Mid-sized Biotech (10-200 employees)
- **Pain Point:** Managing drug safety reporting (ICSRs) without a full-time, expensive PV department.
- **Buying Signals:** Recent Series B/C funding, new clinical trial launch, or expansion into new clinical regions.
- **Decision Maker:** Head of Clinical Operations, Chief Medical Officer, or VP of Regulatory Affairs.

## Segment 2: CROs (Contract Research Organizations)
- **Pain Point:** Inconsistent narrative quality across diverse drug safety projects.
- **Buying Signals:** Expanding service offerings, hiring new PV associates.
- **Decision Maker:** Director of Pharmacovigilance, PV Operations Manager.

## Behavioral Filtering
- **Prioritize:** Companies posting about "regulatory inspection readiness" or "scaling safety operations."
- **Ignore:** Large Pharma (unless specific divisions are identified).
